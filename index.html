03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Ashley Ju ↗

I’m a UNC–Chapel Hill senior drawn to the mix of research, creativity, and problem-solving behind effective advertising and communications: understanding an audience, finding the right message, and reaching people in ways that matter to them.

In the North Carolina Governor’s Office and the U.S. House of Representatives, I’ve written public-facing communications and researched complex issues for different audiences. As a contributing writer for The Carrboro Defender, I bring together reporting, evidence, and personal perspective to make local issues clear and compelling. These experiences have strengthened my written and verbal communication skills and my ability to tailor a message to different audiences.

I put my creative interests into practice through digital content. At Raleigh Boots on the Ground, I managed digital outreach and a $25K budget, helping increase engagement by 218%. My food-focused TikTok, @ashjueats, earned 100K+ likes in three months. These experiences fuel my interest in how storytelling, audience insight, and digital platforms can help brands connect with people.

My sales experience adds a practical customer perspective. At Club Pilates, I generated $50K+ in membership sales through consultative conversations and targeted outreach. Listening to customers, addressing their questions, and explaining the value of a membership have strengthened my interpersonal communication and problem-solving skills. I’m excited to bring that understanding of customer needs to advertising and communications work that pairs strong writing with thoughtful research and measurable results.

Research shapes how I approach a communication challenge. Through my Wake County public policy capstone as a consulting team member, I’m developing recommendations and benchmarks to strengthen public engagement. My congressional research on issues from artificial intelligence to economic policy has also taught me to get up to speed quickly, identify what matters, and turn information into a useful recommendation.
